Register your organisation for the Developer Hub

Who should register your organisation

Your organisation's Senior Responsible Individual (SRI) must complete the registration.

What an SRI is

The SRI is the most senior person responsible and accountable for your organisation's software and compliance with HMRC's Terms of use (opens in a new tab).

What the SRI is accountable for

If issues arise with your organisation's software or compliance activities, your SRI is the person accountable for how those activities are managed.

Your SRI does not need to manage these activities day-to-day. However, they must be the most senior person accountable for them.

Choosing your SRI

When identifying your SRI, you should:

  • identify who is responsible and accountable within your organisation
  • choose the person who would be accountable if issues arise
  • make sure your choice reflects how your organisation operates

Your SRI will usually be:

  • a board level individual or someone in an equivalent position
  • a person one level below the most senior management team

HMRC engagement

Your SRI should be the person HMRC contacts if we need to discuss issues with your organisation's software or compliance activities. You can nominate other people as operational or day-to-day contacts.

Being named as an SRI does not make someone personally liable for HMRC sanctions or penalties.
