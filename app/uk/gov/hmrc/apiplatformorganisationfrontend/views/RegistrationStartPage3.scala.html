Register your organisation

Before you start

To register your organisation, you'll need:

  • details of the SRI completing the registration
  • information about your organisation

Organisation information

All organisations must provide:

  • organisation type
  • registered organisation name
  • registered address
  • contact details

You may also need to provide:

  • a company registration number (UK incorporated companies)
  • a Unique Taxpayer Reference (UTR) (UK organisations)
  • a tax registration document from the country where the organisation is registered (non-UK organisations)
