What is organisation registration

Organisation registration allows HMRC to verify your organisation before you can access APIs in the production enviroment.

During registration, your Senior Responsible Individual (SRI) provides information to help HMRC verify your organisation.

Benefits of organisation registration

Once you have registered, you can:

  • transfer ownership of applications from individual users to your organisation
  • manage your organisation's applications in one place
  • add users to your organisation and manage their access permissions
